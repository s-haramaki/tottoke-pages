Tottoke Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

Tottoke (the "App") helps you keep videos and growth records of your child on your own device.

This English text is provided for convenience. The Japanese version is the original.

1. Information the App handles

  • Profile name, date of birth, and profile icon
  • Recorded video and audio
  • Prompts, capture subjects, recording dates, and age in months at the time of recording
  • Notification settings and in-app record management data

2. Where data is stored, and external transmission

This information is stored in the App's own storage area on your device and is not sent to any server operated by the developer. The App uses no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no third-party analytics SDKs, and no behavioral tracking. Depending on your device and iCloud backup settings, app data may be included in backups provided by Apple.

To monitor stability, diagnostic information such as crashes, hangs, and launch times is kept on the device for a limited period. This diagnostic information is never sent anywhere outside the device, including to the developer.

3. Exports you choose to make

Only when you explicitly ask for it, the App exports a copy of a video to the Photos app, with the caption and a small icon plus the word "Tottoke" rendered into it. The original and finished videos inside the App are not modified. The App never exports anything automatically.

4. Device features the App uses

  • Camera: to record video
  • Microphone: to record audio together with video
  • Add to Photos: to save a video you selected to the Photos app
  • Notifications: to suggest when to record next. If you turn on showing the capture subject, a prompt may appear on the Lock Screen. This is off by default, and a child's name is never shown in notifications
  • Home Screen widget: to show the day's capture subject and the age label. A child's name, videos, and thumbnails are never shown in the widget

5. Deleting information

You can delete records inside the App. Deleting a person removes them from the candidates for new recordings and notifications. So that past videos are not lost and person tags remain visible, the link between person information and past videos is kept on the device. A person can be restored from Settings. Deleting the App from your device normally deletes the information in its storage area as well, so export any videos you want to keep beforehand.

6. Keeping your data, and backups

The App stores videos and records only on your device. The developer holds no copy of them and cannot restore them if they are lost.

  • The device is lost, damaged, or reset
  • The App is deleted
  • The operating system or hardware fails
  • You delete the data yourself

In these cases the data is lost. Please keep iCloud Backup enabled on your device, or export videos from the library to the Photos app from time to time. Creating, keeping, and restoring backups is your responsibility.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, the developer is not liable for any loss of or damage to data.

7. Children's information

The App has no mechanism to collect information online from a child, and no mechanism to provide a child's information to third parties.

8. Changes and contact

If the features or the handling of information change, this policy will be updated. For inquiries, please use the support contact shown on the App Store product page, or, while the App is distributed through TestFlight, the feedback function in the TestFlight app.
